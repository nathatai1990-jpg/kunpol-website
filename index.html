Group Overview

Built for
the long term

Founded in 1999 by the Temtanakitpaisan family in Khon Kaen, KUNPOL Group began as a single PTT oil station. Over 25 years, it has quietly expanded into six distinct sectors — energy, agriculture, commerce, construction, services, and concrete production — each run with full operational independence, bound by a shared discipline of patient capital and regional commitment.

Governance

Four principles that guide every decision we make.

01
Stewardship
We manage resources — capital, land, people — as temporary custodians responsible to future generations.
02
Discipline
We decline more opportunities than we accept. Discipline means knowing what we do well and staying within it.
03
Accountability
Every subsidiary has a clear mandate. Every manager is measured. We do not tolerate ambiguity in responsibility.
04
Consistency
We apply the same principles across all sectors, all cycles, and all conditions. Trust is built through repetition.
Privately Held

Family ownership.
Generational continuity.

KUNPOL Group is fully owned and managed by the Temtanakitpaisan family. There are no external shareholders, and no intent to list. Our private structure allows us to make decisions based on long-term principle, not short-term market pressure.

The family remains actively involved in the governance and strategic direction of every subsidiary — this is not passive ownership, but committed stewardship.
