About Us

A Diversified
Group Built For
The Long Term

Founded in 1999 by the Temtanakitpaisan family in Khon Kaen, KUNPOL Group began as a single PTT oil station. Over 25 years, it has quietly expanded into six distinct sectors — energy, agriculture, commerce, construction, services, and concrete production.
