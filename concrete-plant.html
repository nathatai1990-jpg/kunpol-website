Sector Overview

The Foundation
Beneath Everything

"Before anything is built — a road, a building, a bridge — someone had to pour the foundation. That is what we do."

KUNPOL's Concrete Plant Division operates ready-mix concrete production facilities in Khon Kaen, supplying consistent, quality-controlled concrete to construction projects across the province and wider region.

As KUNPOL's Construction Division grew, the need for a reliable internal concrete supply became clear. The Concrete Plant was established to serve that need — and has since become a standalone supplier to government contracts, private developers, and third-party contractors as well.

Capabilities & Services

What We Produce
and Deliver

01
Structural Ready-Mix Concrete

High-strength ready-mix concrete produced to specification for structural applications — foundations, columns, slabs, and beams. Available in grades C20 through C40, tested and certified to Thai Industrial Standards for every batch.

02
Road & Pavement Concrete

Specially formulated concrete mixes for road surface applications, including high-durability pavement concrete for government road projects and site access roads requiring heavy load tolerance and long service life.

03
Mobile Pump Truck Service

On-site concrete pumping service using our fleet of mobile pump trucks, allowing concrete to be placed at height or in locations inaccessible to transit mixers. Essential for multi-storey construction and confined site conditions.

04
Bulk Supply Contracts

Long-term supply agreements for large-scale projects requiring consistent, high-volume concrete delivery over extended periods. Pricing stability, dedicated scheduling, and priority allocation during peak demand periods.

05
Custom Mix Design

Bespoke concrete mix design for specialist applications — including waterproof concrete, fibre-reinforced mixes, and rapid-hardening formulations for time-critical construction environments.

Track Record

Milestones in
Concrete Supply

2012
Batching Plant Commissioned

The first KP Ready-Mix batching plant was commissioned in Mueang District, initially serving as an internal supply facility for KUNPOL's own construction projects across the province.

2016
External Supply Operations Begin

Following strong internal demand, the plant expanded capacity and began accepting third-party orders. Within the first year of external operations, the plant had secured supply contracts with five major regional contractors.

2022
Fleet Expansion & Pump Service Launch

The logistics subsidiary expanded its transit mixer fleet to 12 vehicles and added three mobile pump trucks, significantly increasing the division's ability to service high-rise and confined-site construction projects across Khon Kaen.
