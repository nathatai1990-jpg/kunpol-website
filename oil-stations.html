Oil Stations

Three PTT-franchised fuel stations operating across Khon Kaen — serving communities, agriculture, and logistics.

Subsidiaries
  • Kunpol Service Co., Ltd.
  • Kunpol Service Nam Phong Co., Ltd.
  • Kunpol Petroleum Co., Ltd.

PTT Franchise Network

All three stations operate under the PTT Public Company Limited franchise — Thailand's national energy company — ensuring consistent product quality, safety standards, and service protocols across every location. Fuel products offered include Diesel B7, Gasohol 91, Gasohol 95, E20, and PTT-branded lubricants.
