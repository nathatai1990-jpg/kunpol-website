Sector Overview

Rooted in the Land,
Growing for Generations

"Agriculture is the original economy of this region. We are here to honour it and grow it with discipline."

KUNPOL's Agricultural Division manages cassava and sugarcane cultivation across thousands of rai in Northeast Thailand. Our operations span the full agricultural cycle — from land preparation and input supply to harvest logistics and regional trading.

Northeast Thailand's fertile plains and reliable monsoon cycles make it one of the country's most productive agricultural regions. KUNPOL has been part of this landscape for over two decades, building relationships with local communities and supply chains that run deep.

Our Subsidiaries

KUNPOL's Agriculture sector operates as a single division — Kunpol Agriculture — handling cultivation and seasonal crop operations across Khon Kaen province.

Capabilities & Services

What Our Agricultural
Division Does

Land Preparation & Soil Management

Full-scale land clearing, deep plowing, and soil enrichment for cassava and sugarcane cultivation to maximise yield potential.

Crop Cycle Planning

Coordinated planting and harvesting schedules aligned with seasonal rainfall patterns and market windows for optimal returns.

Harvest & Post-Harvest Logistics

End-to-end harvest management including machinery coordination, crop transport, and delivery to processing facilities.

Regional Distribution & Trading

Network of buyer relationships with starch factories, sugar mills, and commodity traders across Khon Kaen and Central Thailand.

Track Record

Milestones in the Field

Early 2000s
First Agricultural Plots Established

KUNPOL began its agricultural journey with the first cassava plots in Khon Kaen, establishing farm management practices that still form the backbone of operations today.

2013
Sugarcane Division Launched

Following successful cassava operations, the Group diversified into sugarcane cultivation, entering into long-term supply arrangements with regional sugar refineries.

2020
KP Agricultural Trading Incorporated

The creation of a dedicated trading subsidiary formalised KUNPOL's role as a regional commodity aggregator, expanding relationships with buyers across multiple provinces.
