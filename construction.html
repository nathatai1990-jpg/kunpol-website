Sector Overview

Infrastructure That
Outlasts Any Season

"We do not build for today. Every road, every structure, every foundation we lay is meant to serve the next generation."

KUNPOL's Construction Division has been a trusted partner in regional infrastructure development across Khon Kaen and the broader Northeast. Our teams deliver civil engineering projects, road construction, earthworks, and government-contracted developments with consistent quality and on-schedule delivery.

With deep roots in the region and established relationships with provincial authorities, KUNPOL Construction is positioned to take on both public infrastructure contracts and private development projects of significant scale.

Capabilities & Services

What We Deliver

Road & Highway Construction

Design and build of rural and provincial road networks, including asphalt paving, drainage, and roadside structures to government specification.

Civil & Structural Engineering

Foundation works, reinforced concrete structures, bridges, culverts, and other civil engineering components for public and private projects.

Commercial & Industrial Buildings

Full general contracting for warehouses, factories, community centres, and agricultural storage facilities across the province.

Site Preparation & Earthworks

Large-volume earth removal, grading, compaction, and site levelling operations using a fully owned fleet of modern heavy machinery.

Heavy Equipment Rental

Short and long-term hire of excavators, dump trucks, graders, and compactors to third-party contractors and public agencies.

Government Project Tendering

Established experience submitting and winning provincial government tenders, with a strong track record of contract performance and delivery.
